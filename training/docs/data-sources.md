## 2. Verification Status Summary: What Is Verified vs UNVERIFIED

### 2.1. Formally Verified Facts

1. **Machine-Generated Annotations**: It is conclusively verified via lines 46–52 of `sonnetechnology` README that 100% of the character transcriptions in `sonnetechnology/license-plate-text-recognition-full` were produced by the automated model `microsoft/trocr-large-printed`, rather than by human annotators.
2. **Derivative CC-BY-4.0 Declaration**: The Hugging Face dataset card for `sonnetechnology/license-plate-text-recognition-full` declares Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC-BY-4.0`).
3. **TrOCR Model Code License**: The underlying research repository `microsoft/unilm` containing TrOCR is licensed under the MIT License.
4. **Frozen Test Integrity**: The 322 Vietnamese license plate instances constituting the frozen test benchmark have immutable SHA-256 and perceptual hash digests pinned in `training/data/frozen_test_registry.json`.

### 2.2. Critical Items Remaining UNVERIFIED

1. **Roboflow Upstream License**: The primary licensing terms of the original "Vehicle Registration Plates Dataset" by Augmented Startups on Roboflow Universe remain **`UNVERIFIED`** due to automated blocking (HTTP 403). It is unconfirmed whether the original dataset permitted redistribution under CC-BY-4.0 or whether it imposed Non-Commercial (`NC`) or proprietary restrictions.
2. **Original Data Capture Rights**: There is zero verified provenance regarding where, how, or from whom the original vehicle photographs were acquired (e.g., whether photographs were scraped from public web galleries, captured via street surveillance, or recorded in private facilities).
3. **Human Ground-Truth Accuracy**: Because all labels in the raw dataset were generated by TrOCR, baseline performance numbers represent model agreement with TrOCR rather than true ground-truth accuracy. Syntactic errors such as row 102 (redacted: `51F-******` [photo SHA-256: `5425ce2473621a7341ed48724c0d695f80407e4c6b04f872fc5686c9a4ef905d`], 9 characters with 6 trailing digits) confirm machine transcription artifacts.

## 3. Legal & Intellectual Property Analysis for Model Training & Distribution

### 3.1. Upstream License Inheritance & Copyright Risks

Under intellectual property law and open-source licensing principles:

- **Derivative Works & Attribution**: A derivative dataset (such as `sonnetechnology`) cannot legally grant rights broader than those granted by its upstream source (`keremberke` and Roboflow `augmented-startups`). If the original Roboflow dataset was published under a Non-Commercial license (e.g., `CC-BY-NC-4.0`), downstream claims of `CC-BY-4.0` are legally invalid regarding commercial exploitation.
- **Weights Derived from Machine-Generated Labels**: Machine-generated text annotations produced by an AI model (TrOCR) generally do not attract human copyright protection under prevailing copyright doctrines. However, the input photographic images remain subject to original photographic copyright.
- **Model Weight Copyright**: Weights resulting from fine-tuning on datasets with uncertain root licenses risk copyright infringement claims if distributed publicly or embedded in commercial SaaS products without explicit authorization from primary data holders.

## 4. Personal Data Protection: Decree 13/2023/ND-CP Compliance

### 4.1. Legal Classification of Vehicle License Plates in Vietnam

Under Vietnamese law (Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection, effective July 1, 2023):

1. **Personal Data Status**: Vehicle license plate numbers constitute personal data when they can be associated, directly or indirectly, with an identifiable individual (vehicle owner or driver) through civil registry, traffic management systems, or external surveillance linkage (Article 2, Decree 13/2023/ND-CP).
2. **Third-Party Real Vehicles**: The images in the `sonnetechnology` / `keremberke` datasets depict real motor vehicles operating on public roads or private properties in Vietnam. These individuals did not provide explicit consent for processing, model training, or dataset publication.

### 4.2. Mandatory Data Minimization & Safeguards

To comply with the principles of data minimization and security under Decree 13/2023/ND-CP:

1. **Tightly Bounded Crops Only**: The pipeline must discard all contextual vehicle imagery, driver faces, pedestrians, and surrounding scenery. Only tightly bounded crops ($64 \times 128$ RGB) containing solely the license plate characters may be processed.
2. **No Public Storage of Identifiable Strings**: Real license plate strings, raw manifests, and full-resolution vehicle crops must never be committed to public git repositories or exposed via public endpoints.
3. **Quarantine & Access Isolation**: Any data processed during development must reside in local cache (`PLATE_TRAINING_CACHE`) or private cloud buckets with restricted role-based access control.

## 5. Strategic Options for the Owner

To address the upstream licensing uncertainty and personal data considerations without imposing a unilateral business decision, three distinct options are presented for the Owner:

```text

│                       OWNER STRATEGIC DECISION OPTIONS                      │

│ • Model weights kept   │ • Contact Augmented        │ • Collect images from │
│   strictly internal    │   Startups for written     │   Owner's parking     │

│ • Fast, low friction   │ • Resolves Tier 3 status   │ • 100% legal clarity  │
│ • Zero commercial SaaS │ • Dependent on response    │ • Requires operational│
│   redistribution       │   from third-party         │   labelling pipeline  │
└────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────┘
```

### Option 1: Internal & Research Training Only (Private Weights)

- **Description**: The Owner proceeds with fine-tuning exclusively for internal research, benchmarking, and internal staging environments. The resulting model checkpoints, weights, and training manifests remain strictly private and are never published to public registries (e.g., Hugging Face, public Kaggle, or open GitHub releases).
- **Pros**: Enables immediate progress on OCR algorithm refinement without external dependencies or delays.
- **Cons / Risks**: The trained model cannot be legally distributed or marketed as a public product. Potential liability remains if private weights are ever exposed or utilized in commercial SaaS deployments without clean provenance.

### Option 2: Seek Written Permission from Augmented Startups

- **Description**: The Owner or legal representative formally contacts Augmented Startups (the original author of `augmented-startups/vehicle-registration-plates-trudk` on Roboflow Universe) to obtain written confirmation of commercial rights or an explicit commercial license.
- **Pros**: Formally resolves the `UNVERIFIED` status of Tier 3 and provides legal certainty for downstream commercialization if granted.
- **Cons / Risks**: Response time is uncertain; third party may decline or request commercial licensing fees.

### Option 3: Progressive Replacement with Owner-Owned Camera Data

- **Description**: The Owner initiates an internal data collection program using video streams from operational parking management cameras installed at client facilities. Clear notice is provided to facility users in accordance with Decree 13/2023/ND-CP. Captured plates are annotated via the internal human review kit (`training/docs/labeling-guide.md`) and progressively replace third-party data.
- **Pros**: Establishes 100% uncontested legal ownership, perfect alignment with Decree 13/2023/ND-CP, and superior model adaptation to the exact camera angles, lighting conditions, and resolutions of the production parking system.
- **Cons / Risks**: Requires operational setup time and annotator hours for collecting and verifying proprietary ground-truth labels.
